CUBE CHASE

You are the blue cube. Red cubes pour in from the edges and hunt you down. One touch and you're out. Grab coins, spend them on power-ups, and survive as long as you can. Survive 20 seconds to win, then 30 seconds, 1 minute, and 30 seconds more after that each time. Every loss costs 5 coins.

PRESS START

Move with WASD or the arrow keys. M turns sound effects on or off. Use the joystick under the field, or drag anywhere on the field to steer. Use power-ups with 1–7 or tap them under the field. P pauses.

Shop

Each purchase is one use. Bring them into any map and fire them off when the red cubes close in.

Skins

Change how your cube looks. Buy a skin once and switch to it any time.

ONLINE PLAY

Play with up to 9 friends. Challenge bots: everyone teams up against the bots. Team vs team: Blue against Gold, up to 5 a side. There are no bots: you hunt each other. Everyone gets a free fireball every 8 seconds, a fireball knocks out enemy players, and the last team with someone standing wins. One person creates a room and shares the code.
